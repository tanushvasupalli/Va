## 2026-09-30 - Faster Root Mean Square (RMS) calculation for Audio Stream Processing
**Learning:** In highly frequent audio stream chunk processing (called repeatedly in a tight loop), array-wide exponentiation like `np.mean(chunk ** 2)` is surprisingly slower than using linear algebra utilities `np.linalg.norm(chunk)`. Benchmarking showed that `float(np.linalg.norm(chunk)) / math.sqrt(chunk.size)` runs approximately ~3.5-4x faster than `float(np.sqrt(np.mean(chunk ** 2)))`. Also, substituting `np.sqrt` with `math.sqrt` for scalars helps avoid numpy ufunc dispatch overheads.
**Action:** When calculating RMS over numeric arrays in performance-critical sections, rely on `np.linalg.norm` and native `math.sqrt` instead of naive full-array exponentiation and numpy scalar functions.
